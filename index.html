FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience?

No. Both complete beginners and intermediate learners are welcome. We start from where you are.

Is this 1-on-1 or group?

Both. You get personal 1-on-1 sessions plus access to a small group of peers for accountability and collaboration.

Will I actually build projects?

Absolutely — projects are the core of the program, not an afterthought. You'll finish with 2–3 deployable projects on your GitHub.

How many seats are in a batch?

I keep batches small (4–6 students) to ensure personal attention and mentorship quality.

What are the fees?

Pricing is flexible based on your background and goals. Message me on WhatsApp and I'll share the details — no pressure.

How long is the program?

Typically 12 weeks, though we adjust based on your pace and availability. Part-time and weekend schedules are available.
